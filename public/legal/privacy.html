Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 3/1/2025

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a TotemBound user.

1. What We Collect

TotemBound is a decentralized app. While most interactions occur via blockchain wallets, we collect additional data when you register for a subscription plan:

  • Email address (for account management and communication)
  • Wallet address (used for identity, transactions, and tracking game state)

We may also collect:

  • Device type, browser, and language preference
  • Game session data (Totem species, training history, progress)
  • On-chain transaction metadata

2. Subscription Plans & Payments

TotemBound offers optional subscription tiers that enhance gameplay and provide gasless transaction support:

  • Standard Plan: Sponsored transactions with no gas fees for the user. We may introduce advertising to support this tier in the future. Your email and wallet address are required.
  • Premium Plan: A paid monthly subscription that includes priority support and premium in-game rewards. Payments are processed by a third-party provider, and TotemBound does not store or access your credit card or banking details. Your email and wallet address are required to link the subscription to your TotemBound account.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use data to:

  • Deliver and personalize game features
  • Power sponsored or subscription-based experiences
  • Process and verify subscription access
  • Detect abuse, prevent fraud, and maintain security
  • Improve game design and performance based on analytics

4. Off-Chain Metadata

Some gameplay features use off-chain metadata (e.g. Totem happiness, cooldowns, training stats), linked to your wallet. This data is securely stored and accessed only by our platform for gameplay functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services for:

  • Payment processing (Premium tier)
  • Transaction relays (gasless sponsorship)
  • Email delivery and customer communication
  • Analytics and performance monitoring

These services are contractually obligated to handle your data securely and are used only for the specific purposes listed. We do not sell your data or use it for behavioral advertising.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect data stored off-chain. However, no system is completely secure. You are responsible for the security of your wallet and private keys.

7. Your Rights

You may request to:

  • Access or correct your email and account data
  • Unsubscribe or terminate your Premium plan
  • Request deletion of off-chain gameplay data (where feasible)

To make a request, contact legal@totembound.com.

8. Children

TotemBound is not intended for children under 13. If you are under this age, do not use the platform.

Note: TotemBound requires users to be 18 years or older to access the platform, as outlined in our Terms of Service. This Privacy Policy ensures we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13, in accordance with applicable data protection laws such as COPPA.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted in-app or on our website. Continued use of TotemBound after changes means you accept the new policy terms.
