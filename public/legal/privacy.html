Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a TotemBound user.

1. What We Collect

When you create an account and use TotemBound, we collect:

  • Account information: Email address, display name, and password (stored securely using industry-standard hashing)
  • Game data: Totem collection, progression, achievements, challenge scores, expedition history, and currency balances
  • Device information: Browser type, device type, language preference, and operating system
  • Usage data: Login timestamps, session activity, and feature usage patterns

2. Payments & Purchases

TotemBound offers optional in-app purchases (Gem packs) and subscription plans. Payments are processed by Stripe, a PCI-compliant third-party payment provider. We do not store or have access to your credit card number, banking details, or full payment information. Stripe's privacy policy governs the handling of your payment data.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the game experience
  • Process account registration and authentication
  • Deliver in-game rewards, track progress, and manage subscriptions
  • Send important account notifications (e.g., password resets, security alerts)
  • Detect abuse, prevent fraud, and maintain platform security
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve game design and performance

We do not sell your personal data or use it for behavioral advertising.

4. Infrastructure & Third-Party Services

TotemBound is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS). We use the following services to operate the platform:

  • AWS Cognito: Secure user authentication and account management
  • AWS DynamoDB: Game data storage (totems, progress, achievements)
  • AWS S3 & CloudFront: Static asset delivery and content distribution
  • AWS SES: Transactional email delivery (verification, notifications)
  • AWS IoT Core: Real-time push notifications
  • Stripe: Payment processing for purchases and subscriptions

These services are contractually obligated to handle your data securely and are used only for the purposes listed above.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

TotemBound uses browser local storage to maintain your session and preferences:

  • Authentication tokens: To keep you logged in between visits
  • Game preferences: Theme (light/dark mode), notification settings
  • Cached game state: Currency balances and recent activity for faster loading

We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), secure password hashing, and access controls on our infrastructure. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data and game information
  • Correct inaccurate account information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your game data in a portable format (upon request)
  • Cancel your subscription at any time

To exercise these rights, contact legal@totembound.com.

8. Data Retention

We retain your account and game data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will remove your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate business purposes (e.g., fraud prevention).

9. Children

TotemBound is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 in accordance with COPPA. Users between 13 and 18 should have parental or guardian consent. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted in-app or on our website. Continued use of TotemBound after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions or concerns about your privacy? Contact us at legal@totembound.com.
